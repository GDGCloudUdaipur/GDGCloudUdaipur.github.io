Fateh Sagar Paal

फ़तेह सागर पाल · The Open Ideation Hub

Evening breeze, roadside kulhad chai, and open whiteboards. Udaipur's developers have long gathered by the waters of Fateh Sagar to debate architectures, brainstorm hackathon prototypes, and collaborate beyond classroom walls.

#LakefrontVibes
🏰

City Palace Resilience

सिटी पैलेस · Architectural Scalability

Constructed over 400 years by generations of Mewar rulers, the City Palace stands unshakeable against the tests of time. That same resilience inspires how we design distributed, high-availability, fault-tolerant cloud systems on GCP.

#ResilientSystems
⛰️

Sajjangarh Vision

सज्जनगढ़ · Elevated Horizons

Perched high atop Bansdara Peak, the Monsoon Palace watches over the entire Aravalli skyline. We channel this panoramic vision into frontier tech—deep diving into Gemini 2.0, autonomous AI agents, and next-gen cloud infra.

#FrontierAI
🪔

🏛️ Our Legacy & Mission 🏛️

Rooted in Mewar,
Building for the World

GDG Cloud Udaipur is Rajasthan’s premier community of developers, cloud engineers, AI researchers, and students. Founded in the historic Venice of the East, we combine Rajasthan's timeless traditions of craftsmanship and honour with world-class engineering.

From conducting deep-dive workshops on Google Cloud Platform, Vertex AI, and Kubernetes, to hosting the flagship Cloud Community Days and the Agentic Premier League, our mission is to build local talent into global technology leaders.
