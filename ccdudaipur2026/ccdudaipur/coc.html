Community guidelines

Code of conduct.

Cloud Community Days Udaipur is for everyone who wants to learn and build. We follow the Google Developer Groups community guidelines, and we want every attendee, speaker, volunteer and partner to have a safe, welcoming day free of harassment.

Be kind and inclusive

  • Treat everyone with respect, whatever their gender, identity, age, ability, appearance, background, religion, caste, language or level of experience.
  • Welcome beginners. Every expert in the room was a beginner once.
  • Critique ideas, not people.

What we don't accept

  • Harassment, intimidation, stalking or unwanted photography or recording.
  • Offensive comments or imagery in talks, slides, booths, social posts or chats.
  • Sustained disruption of talks, labs or other activities.
  • Unwelcome physical contact or sexual attention.

If something happens

Find any organiser or volunteer in a team T-shirt, or visit the registration desk. We will listen, keep it confidential, and act. Organisers may warn, remove from the event without refund, or report to authorities where needed.

Photos

The event is photographed and filmed. If you'd rather not appear, ask for a no-photo lanyard at registration and our crew will respect it.
