A DAY IN UDAIPUR06:00
Learn it 06:00 · Gangaur Ghat

Learn it at first light.

Mist lifts off Pichola, the aarti lamps burn down on the ghat, and the day opens with deep dives on Gemini, agentic AI, data and cloud architecture from people who run these systems for a living.

  • Gemini & multimodal
  • Agents with ADK
  • Data that talks back
Build it 12:30 · Over the Old City

Build it by noon.

Kites over the rooftops, laptops open below. Follow-along Build Labs take you from an empty project to a deployed app on Cloud Run and Firebase, with Cloud credits on us and mentors walking the rows.

  • Hands-on labs
  • Credits included
  • Mentors on the floor
Ship it 17:30 · Sajjangarh ridge

Ship it by sunset.

As the ropeway carries the last cabins up Machla Magra and the sun drops behind the Monsoon Palace, the best Rapid Hack teams and Code for Communities winners demo what they built.

  • Rapid Hacks
  • Live demos
  • Prizes
Celebrate it 20:00 · Lake Pichola

Celebrate it under the lights.

December in Udaipur: the palace glows gold, diyas drift on the water and wedding fireworks break over the lake. We end with awards, a group photo and a long walk by the water talking about what you'll build next.

  • Awards
  • Community
  • Chai by the lake
